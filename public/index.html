            How it works

            Watching Microsoft

            Every five minutes the site checks the Microsoft Security Update Guide. When Microsoft publishes or revises a bulletin, a pipeline re-reads the release, compares it with the previous one and records what changed. Open pages update by themselves.

            Deciding what to patch first

            Each CVE gets a priority from Microsoft's severity, the CVSS score, evidence of exploitation, the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and the FIRST EPSS probability. The priority sets a patch-by date.

            Why it matters for NIS2

            Article 21(2)(e) of the NIS2 Directive, transposed in Italy by Legislative Decree 138/2024, asks for a documented, risk-based approach to vulnerability handling. The exported queue is evidence of that process.

            Data: Microsoft Security Update Guide, CISA KEV, FIRST EPSS. An independent project by Luisa Mele, not affiliated with Microsoft.
