    IT

    Get told when it matters, and only then.

    Free alerts on Telegram for Patch Tuesday, Microsoft vulnerabilities newly exploited, updates Microsoft asks to reinstall, CSIRT Italia bulletins and any CVE or update you choose. A quiet week sends nothing.

    On Telegram

    Open the bot and send /watch exploited, /watch acn or /watch KB5070112. Only your Telegram chat id and the topics you choose are stored; /stop deletes everything.

    In this browser

    Use Follow on any CVE or update page. While the site is open you get an on-page notice, and a desktop alert if you turn them on, when something you follow changes.

    In your feed reader or calendar

    RSS feeds for updates, exploited vulnerabilities, Microsoft changes and CSIRT Italia, plus a calendar of the next Patch Tuesdays.

    See feeds and API

    Browser notifications

    Notifications from this site even when it is closed, on computer and phone. Free, no account: the site stores only an anonymous address created by your browser and the topics you choose. On iPhone and iPad, first add the site to the Home Screen.
